How It Works
Set it once. The AI handles the rest.
Three simple inputs. Infinite intelligence behind the scenes.
1

Tell It Where

Enter your destination and what time you need to arrive. Add your get-ready time (shower, coffee, etc).

2

AI Calculates

The AI analyzes real-time traffic, weather forecasts, and your personal patterns to compute the optimal wake time.

3

It Adapts Live

While you sleep, the alarm continuously updates. If a highway closes at 3 AM, your alarm moves earlier. You wake up right on time.

4

It Learns You

Over time, the AI tracks whether you're consistently late or early and adjusts future alarms to match your actual behavior.
